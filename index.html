    How the demo calculates
    • Demand charge €100 per kW and year. Load management cuts the peak by 40% of the flexible share, at most 15%.
    • Load shifting moves 25% of the flexible energy to cheaper quarter hours at a €45 per MWh spread. It needs a dynamic power contract.
    • Efficiency saves 10% of the flexible load plus 2% of the rest, at €450 invested per MWh saved each year.
    • The battery covers 15% of the peak for two hours at €350 per kWh installed, with 300 cycles a year at a €60 per MWh spread.
    • Load management costs €40k per plant plus €10k per MW of peak; the shifting software €60k.
    • Typical ranges: price from BDEW 2026 averages by consumption size; peak from typical operating hours of the plant type; electricity use and flexible share are rough estimates from public energy guides (cooling is about 40% of brewery electricity, around 50% in meat processing).
    • Data centers: cooling and flexible AI workloads form the flexible share; efficiency saves 8% of it plus 1% of the rest; 15% of it shifts; load management caps at most 4% of the peak; the battery covers 20% of the peak.
    • CO₂ at 0.36 t per MWh of electricity saved. The curve is a modelled working day of a food plant, scaled to your values.
